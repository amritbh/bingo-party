How It Works

🖨️

Step 1: Print Cards

Generate 200 unique Dashain-themed bingo cards. Print them on color paper for the best festive look!

🎫

Step 2: Distribute

Give 4 cards to each of the 50 players. Each card has unique numbers!

📺

Step 3: Call Numbers

Use the PC/projector to display the random number caller. Works on iPhone too!

🏆

Step 4: Win!

First to complete a line shouts "BINGO!" and receives Dashain Ashirbad!
